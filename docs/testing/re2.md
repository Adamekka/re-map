## Door unlock state

[RE2-DOOR-001](../research/re2.md#re2-door-001-event-driven-unlock-and-savestate-reversal) records one event-driven bank 31 bit 17 unlock. Ten accepted case captures verify the native flag change, paired current-door states, denied passage before activation, passage in both directions, reentry, actual earlier/later loads and a repeated native unlock. Three additional setup captures are listed separately. The earlier and later restores reproduce their original RAM bytes. A departed door retains only fresh unlock knowledge and loses that label after reversal. A separate production history-file round trip reconstructs both outcomes from the real captures without persisting access verdicts.

The unchanged reader passed the 89-case focused RE2 suite. This case required no behavior change. Use the research entry's checkpoint identities, position/floor patches, accepted input sequence and exclusions for reproduction. It does not validate a named key, operating a one-sided unlock handler, natural floor transitions, ordinary navigation or memory-card saves.

## Named-key unlocking

[RE2-DOOR-002](../research/re2.md#re2-door-002-named-key-use-and-savestate-reversal) records one native key pickup/use, denied passage without the key, carrying the key while the door remains Locked, the unlock message and completed use, paired passage and reentry. Actual earlier/later savestate loads reverse and restore acquisition, key quantity and unlock state while retaining visits. A second native use after an earlier load reproduces the unlock. No tracker behavior change was needed.

The private audit replays 25 accepted captures through the unchanged production reader. It checks four Retry captures for unchanged publication, three byte-identical restore comparisons, removal/restoration of the door's key label, departed-door uncertainty and a separate nine-room/nine-region history-file round trip. These controlled cases add eight previously uncaptured sources, bringing the recorded runtime-source union to nineteen. This measures accepted source reads, not complete item/door coverage or campaign readiness.

Use the focused RE2 command above for existing flag, source-binding and publication regressions. Preparation used recorded position/floor edits, native item interactions and inherited health/ammunition assistance. All direct writes were limited to the recorded position/floor fields. The isolated emulator was stopped and its original settings and test slot restored. Follow the research claim for capture hashes, exact setup, excluded attempts and remaining limits.
